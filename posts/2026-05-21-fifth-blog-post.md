## Performance Testing
As we know from the initial brief our hub's load time has to be ideally under 1 second with a maximum of 3 seconds, to test this we will plug our website into a free diagnostic tool to find out the speed and any usability bottlenecks that may be occurring from images, database calls or unnecessary scripts. Before testing we are currently concerned about the load time for the feed since the database will be fetching quite a lot of pitches at once which leaves us with uncertainty and caution as to how this may affect the speed of our hub.

## Usability Testing
To then test the overall usability of our hub I have thought of a few tasks / scenarios that our group could possibly use to get rich user data before publishing our hub
Suppose you need help to build this product you came up with an idea for. Find someone to help you create this idea.
- Suppose you are a junior developer wanting to get experience, browse the hub to find a project you are interested in
- Suppose your post has received several applications. Review the applicants and manage your team
- Suppose you have written the brand messaging, find tasks that are outstanding and check ones that you have completed.
- Suppose you are browsing the showcase and found work that you were impressed with, show the community what you thought of it.
The tasks I came up with are actionable, yet are not too leading. So by testing these tasks our hub will be successful if they can be completed without confusion or needing further instruction. 

## Accessibility Evaluation
Before publishing our hub we also need to ensure we at least comply with AA requirements, so we will have to put our website into the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines page to check overall that the titles, paragraphs, structure and colours all meet the requirements. Whilst also ensuring specific things like form labels on our pitch submission page, colour contrast on the progress bar also meet the AA requirements.

## Responsible Design and Compliance
Throughout the process of creating our hub we have had to think about many ethical considerations when it comes to our users' data. Firstly as the brief states we need to comply with EU cookie consent, so we know session cookies are essential so technically we don’t need consent for that but if we decide to look at analytics from our users we must think about how we notify and confirm consent. With the data we are storing like usernames, role descriptions, ratings, etc are all crucial to our hub functioning successfully so it will be necessary for us to collect the data. However we don’t want users to feel like we are ‘stealing’ their data so to avoid this we aim to be transparent and open with our community in what their data is being used for. When thinking about our showcase we raised a small ethical consideration in terms of the badge system. As badges are publicly visible and tied to community ratings we are wondering how we could prevent a group of friends inflating each other’s ratings fairly. Though we have not thought of a solution yet, this is something we are aware of and are going to be careful of before publishing our hub.
